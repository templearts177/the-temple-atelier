Choose a treatment

Three approaches.

Each is a complete artistic treatment—not a good/better/best quality ladder.

Linear Study

A line-led portrait built through deliberate strokes and hatching. Expressive, precise and visibly drawn.

Classic Portrait

Fuller tonal modelling, developed shading and smoother transitions for greater depth and realism.

Premium Realism

The most intensive treatment, with highly developed detail, texture, tonal depth and refined realism.

How it works

Simple by design.

Choose → Send reference → 50% deposit → Drawing → Review → Balance → Delivery

Reference

Send a clear, well-lit photograph with visible facial detail and minimal filtering. Strong reference material helps produce a stronger portrait.

Payment

A 50% deposit confirms the commission. The remaining balance is due on completion before delivery. One revision is included before final delivery.

Framing

Framing is optional and priced separately. Commission clients receive 35% off the standard framing price.
